Your information

Privacy Policy

Draft. This policy describes how the site currently works and has not yet had legal review. It will be updated, with a dated change note, before partner data or real users are accepted at scale.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address, password (stored only as a hash by our authentication provider) and the display name you choose.
  • Saved places: the listings you save to your account.
  • Enquiries: your name, email, country, travel dates, party size and message when you contact a business.
  • Business submissions: business and listing details submitted by tourism businesses, including public contact details.
  • Reports: any report you submit about a listing.
  • Contact messages: the name, email address, topic and message you send through the contact form.

Browsing destinations and guides does not require an account. We do not process bookings or payments.

How we use it

  • To run your account, show your saved places and let you sign in or reset your password.
  • To pass your enquiry to the business you contacted so they can reply.
  • To read and reply to messages you send us.
  • To review and moderate business profiles, listings and reports, and to keep the platform safe from spam and abuse.

Who can see it

  • The business you enquire with can see the details in that enquiry. Do not include information you are not comfortable sharing with them.
  • Platform administrators can see account, business, enquiry and report records as needed for moderation and support.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Service providers and third parties

  • Supabase hosts our database and authentication.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr (a script library) and Unsplash (photographs) are loaded from their servers when you open a page, so those providers can receive your IP address and browser details under their own policies.

The site does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser’s local storage by our authentication library.

Retention and your rights

We keep account and enquiry records while your account is active or as needed for the purposes above. Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. To make a request, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”. Self-service account deletion and data export are planned but not yet available.

Security

Data access is restricted with database row-level security so people see only the records they are entitled to. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: use the contact form.
