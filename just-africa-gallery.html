Okavango Delta

Wildlife in Botswana

A vast inland delta of channels and islands, explored by boat, traditional canoe or on foot.

Fauna: elephants, lions, hippos, African wild dogs, red lechwe and more than 400 bird species.

Flora: papyrus, water lilies and palm-fringed islands.

Chobe National Park

Wildlife in Botswana

Famous for river safaris, where large herds come down to the water in the dry season.

Fauna: one of Africa's largest elephant populations, plus buffalo, lions, hippos and giraffe.

Flora: floodplain grasses with mopane and Kalahari teak woodland.

Makgadikgadi Pans

Wildlife in Botswana

Huge salt pans with a wide open sky, best for quiet, remote stays.

Fauna: zebra migration in the wet season, flamingos on the pans and habituated meerkats.

Flora: ancient baobabs, palm islands and open grass plains.

Sossusvlei
