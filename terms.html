What Just Africa is

Booking requests

When you request a stay, you are asking the property to host you on the dates and rooms you choose. A request is not a confirmed booking: the property may confirm or decline it, and rooms are held only once it confirms. The price shown is an estimate calculated from the property’s published rate; the property confirms the final price. Your stay is an arrangement between you and the property, which is responsible for the accommodation, its terms, payment arrangements and any cancellation or refund policy it publishes. You can cancel a request or confirmed booking from your dashboard; the property’s own cancellation terms may still apply.
